Privacy policy

Data controller

The controller of the data collected through the website www.ondeo-agency.com is the company ONDEO, a SASU whose registered office is located at 60 rue François Ier, 75008 Paris, represented by Robin Maxime Paris.

Contact: robinparis.contact@gmail.com

Data collected and purposes

The website has neither a customer area nor a contact form. Data may be collected in the following cases:

  • Booking an appointment. When you book a time slot through the Calendly calendar, the information you enter in this module (for example your name and email address) is sent to ONDEO in order to organize the appointment and get back to you about your project.
  • Browsing the website. Displaying the website involves the transmission of technical data (in particular your IP address) to our hosting provider and to the third-party services required for it to work, listed below.
  • Audience measurement and advertising. If you consent to it, cookies and trackers are used to measure the website’s audience and the effectiveness of our advertising campaigns.

Legal bases

  • Booking an appointment: pre-contractual measures taken at your request.
  • Operation and security of the website: ONDEO’s legitimate interest in providing a working website.
  • Audience measurement and advertising trackers: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

Third-party services used

  • Vercel (Vercel Inc.): website hosting. Vercel processes the technical connection data required to deliver the website.
  • Google Fonts (Google): display of the website’s fonts. Loading the fonts involves the transmission of your IP address to Google’s servers. No cookie is set by our website on this occasion.
  • Vimeo: playback of the website’s videos. The players are embedded with the “Do Not Track” option offered by Vimeo in order to limit tracking. Displaying them involves the transmission of technical data (in particular your IP address) to Vimeo.
  • Calendly: online appointment booking. The calendar is only loaded if you have accepted the “Analytics” category or if you ask for it to be displayed. Calendly then sets its own cookies and processes the information you enter.
  • Google Tag Manager (Google): tool for managing audience measurement and advertising tags. It is only loaded after you consent to at least one optional category, and its tags only fire for the categories you have accepted.
  • Meta Pixel (Meta): tracking of our advertising campaigns on Meta’s services. Loaded only if you accept the “Marketing” category.
  • LinkedIn Insight Tag (LinkedIn): tracking of our advertising campaigns on LinkedIn. Loaded only if you accept the “Marketing” category.

Each of these providers processes data according to its own privacy policy. Some are established outside the European Union, in particular in the United States: your data may therefore be transferred outside the European Union, under the conditions set by these providers.

Cookies and trackers

On your first visit, a banner lets you accept, reject or customize the use of cookies and trackers, which are divided into three categories:

  • Necessary (always active): operation of the website and remembering your cookie choice.
  • Analytics: audience measurement when it is used, and automatic display of the Calendly calendar.
  • Marketing: Meta Pixel, LinkedIn Insight Tag and advertising tags loaded via Google Tag Manager.

Optional categories are disabled by default. Your choice is saved in your browser and kept for 6 months, after which you are asked again.
